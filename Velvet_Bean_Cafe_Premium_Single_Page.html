Our story

Built around the ritual of a really good cup.

Velvet Bean started with a simple idea: make a café where people can slow down. We partner with small roasters, local bakers and farmers whenever possible, then turn those ingredients into honest food and memorable drinks.

Come in for ten quiet minutes or stay for three hours. There is always another cup waiting.
